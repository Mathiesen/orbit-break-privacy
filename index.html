Orbitune – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

Orbitune is a game made by Ib Kunz Mathiesen (Denmark). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it on Android, in a web browser, or on desktop.

The short version

Orbitune itself does not collect any personal data. There are no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only exception is the optional online leaderboards on Android, which run through Google Play Games (see below).

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things locally on your own device so it can remember them between sessions:

  • your high scores and which stages you have cleared
  • your settings (volume, size, screen shake, flashing, vibration, music on/off)

This data stays on your device. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Android: Settings → Apps → Orbitune → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the game.

Online leaderboards (Android only)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game sends your score to a Google Play Games leaderboard when a run ends, and shows the top scores and your own rank. The leaderboard shows your Play Games player name and score to other players. This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; we only see the public leaderboard, the same as everyone else.

If you are not signed in to Play Games, nothing is sent. You can sign out of Play Games or change who can see your profile in the Google Play Games app or settings. The web and desktop versions have no online leaderboards.

Permissions

On Android the game may use vibration for haptic feedback. You can turn this off in the game's settings. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Third parties

Apart from Google Play Games leaderboards on Android (described above), the game contains no third-party services that collect data. If you download it from a store (such as Google Play or itch.io), that store may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game itself does not collect any data from anyone, including children. Google Play Games has its own rules for younger users.

Changes

If the game starts collecting more data (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date at the top will change.
