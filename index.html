Orbit Break – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

Orbit Break is a game made by Ib Kunz Mathiesen (Denmark). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it on Android, in a web browser, or on desktop.

The short version

Orbit Break does not collect, send or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things locally on your own device so it can remember them between sessions:

  • your high scores and which stages you have cleared
  • your settings (volume, size, screen shake, flashing, vibration, music on/off)

This data never leaves your device. We cannot see it. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Android: Settings → Apps → Orbit Break → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the game.

Permissions

On Android the game may use vibration for haptic feedback. You can turn this off in the game's settings. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party services that collect data. If you download it from a store (such as Google Play or itch.io), that store may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game does not collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If the game ever starts collecting data (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date at the top will change.
