Orbitune – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Orbitune is a game made by Ib Kunz Mathiesen (Denmark). This policy explains what happens to your data when you play it on Android, in a web browser, or on desktop.

The short version

Orbitune itself does not collect any personal data, has no analytics and runs no servers of our own. On Android the free game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects some data to show and measure ads (see below). The optional Google Play Games features (leaderboards, achievements and cloud save) and purchases through Google Play are also handled by Google. The web and desktop versions have no ads.

What is stored on your device

The game saves a few things locally on your own device so it can remember them between sessions:

  • your high scores, which stages you have cleared, and simple game statistics (such as runs played and stardust collected)
  • the trails, skins, suns and sounds you have unlocked or bought, and your stardust balance
  • your settings (volume, size, screen shake, flashing, vibration, music on/off)

We cannot see this data. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data (Android: Settings → Apps → Orbitune → Storage → Clear data) or by uninstalling the game.

Google Play Games (Android only)

On Android, if you are signed in to Google Play Games, the game uses it for three things:

  • Leaderboards: when a run ends, your score is sent to a leaderboard, and the game shows the top scores and your own rank. The leaderboard shows your Play Games player name and score to other players.
  • Achievements: achievements you earn are unlocked on your Play Games profile.
  • Cloud save: the game data listed above (except some settings that belong to each device) is saved to your Google account, so your progress follows you to a new phone.

This data is handled by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We only see the public leaderboards, the same as everyone else; we have no access to your Google account. Google Play Games may also collect diagnostic data about its own service.

If you are not signed in to Play Games, nothing is sent. You can sign out of Play Games or change who can see your profile in the Google Play Games settings, and you can delete your Play Games data for this game from your Play Games profile or your Google account. The web and desktop versions have none of these online features.

Ads (Android only)

The Android version shows ads from Google AdMob: an occasional full-screen ad between runs, and optional ads you choose to watch for a reward (such as continuing a run or doubling your stardust). To show, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID, approximate location (based on your IP address), information about your device, and how you interact with the ads. This data goes directly to Google; we only see anonymous totals, such as how many ads were shown.

Your choice (EU, UK and Switzerland): when you start the game for the first time, you are asked whether you consent to personalised ads. Without consent you get non-personalised ads. You can change your choice at any time under Settings → Ad privacy → CHANGE. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

How Google uses this data is described in the Google ad partner policy and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases (Android only)

You can buy stardust, a supporter pack, and an option to remove ads. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play; we never see your payment details. The game only stores what you have bought, and asks Google Play to restore your purchases if you reinstall.

Sharing your score

The SHARE button on the game-over screen makes a picture of your run with your score. The picture is made on your device and only goes where you choose to send it, through your device's own sharing options. We do not receive it.

Permissions

On Android the game may use vibration for haptic feedback (you can turn this off in the game's settings), and it uses the internet and the advertising ID for ads. The game does not access your camera, microphone, contacts, precise location or files.

Third parties

Apart from Google AdMob, Google Play Games and Google Play purchases on Android (described above), the game contains no third-party services that collect data. If you download it from a store (such as Google Play or itch.io), that store may collect data under its own privacy policy.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. The game itself does not collect any data from anyone. Ads are provided by Google AdMob, and Google Play Games and Google Play have their own rules for younger users.

Changes

If the game starts collecting more data, this policy will be updated before that change is released, and the date at the top will change.
